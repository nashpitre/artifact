# Artifact: visual direction

Quiet surfaces. Brief disruptions.

Artifact treats distortion as a recognizable gesture: a clean composition briefly breaks, then settles. The effect should feel incidental, like an imperfect transmission.

Start with warm paper or near-black, readable type, thin rules, and one signal color. Use a large, plain wordmark: ARTIFACT. A small offset fragment can carry the interruption into static work. Keep the normal state strong enough to stand alone.

On the web, leave long quiet gaps between 220 ms disturbances. In motion work, use the same return to clarity. In print, use a displaced line or fragment. In an installation, let one brief interruption travel across otherwise calm surfaces.

These are starting directions, not requirements for users of the code. Bring your own identity. Artifact's consistent gesture is the rhythm.
